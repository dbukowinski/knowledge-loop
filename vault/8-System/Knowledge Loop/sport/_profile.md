# Domain profile: sport

## Scope
Running (trail, ultra), gym, recovery, training plans on the watch platform.

## Observation sources (cost ladder)
1. Watch platform summaries: training load, recovery, HRV and sleep, resting HR, activity list.
2. Session notes in the `Sport` folder (feel, RPE, gym, weights), daily plan.
3. Lap data — key sessions only, max 2 per week.
4. FIT files — only when a hypothesis needs the detail.

`source_id`: `coros:<activity_id>` for activities, `vault:<path>` for session notes.

## Guiding methodology
Evoke Endurance / Scott Johnston (Uphill Athlete) — an interpretive frame, not absolute truth. Knowledge base: wiki `4-Knowledge/Sport & Training` + Evoke PDFs.

## Measurement notes (mountain terrain)
- Pace and HR from mountain runs are misleading: elevation, descents, heat and muscular fatigue change the picture.
- Compare only similar sessions (terrain, elevation, temperature, session type). Otherwise the observation is `contaminated`.
- Aerobic base markers (HR drift, aerobic threshold) — best from flat terrain or a treadmill in stable conditions.

## Confounders checklist
Short sleep, work stress, calorie deficit, terrain/elevation/weather/heat, infection, day after gym, days since last hard session, travel/family weekend.

## Requires approval (→ `_proposals.md`)
- Rules that increase volume or intensity, change the distribution of hard sessions, or the taper.
- Changes to facts that zones and thresholds depend on (aerobic threshold, lactate threshold, max HR).

## Safety
Pain, infection, overload signals (falling HRV + rising resting HR + feeling unwell) beat any rule. Health, injuries and medication — for a specialist, not for plan changes.
